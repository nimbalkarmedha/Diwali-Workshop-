What kids will make

Diya Painting, Toran, Festive Candle Holder, Paper Lantern, Wall Hanging and Greeting Card. 6 items in total.

How each item is made

Tap an item to see the steps. Your child does it step by step with guidance.

Diya Painting
  • Paint a base color on the diya
  • Add dots, flowers and patterns
  • Outline with gold and add glitter
  • Dry and take home, ready to light
Toran
  • Cut flower and leaf shapes
  • Stick them on a strip
  • Add beads and bells
  • Hang it on your door
Festive Candle Holder
  • Decorate the outside with colors and patterns
  • Add stickers and beads
  • Place a tea-light inside
Paper Lantern
  • Fold and cut the paper
  • Roll and stick into a lantern shape
  • Add a handle and decorations
Wall Hanging
  • Paint or color the background
  • Stick the festive designs
  • Add ribbon to hang on the wall
Greeting Card
  • Fold the card and draw a Diwali design
  • Decorate and write a wish
  • Give it to family or friends
